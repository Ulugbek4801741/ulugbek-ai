# ULUGBEK AI — Cost and Resource Governance

Track revenue separately from gross profit and net profit. Record hosting, storage, bandwidth, taxes, refunds, payment fees, contractor costs and AI/API costs. Enforce explicit budgets and hard spending ceilings. Free-tier limits and provider quotas must be treated as variable, not guaranteed. Never upgrade a server, buy storage or subscribe to a service without owner approval.
