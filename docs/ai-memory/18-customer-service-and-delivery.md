# ULUGBEK AI — Customer Service and Delivery

For each paid or free service task:
1. Clarify the requested deliverable and acceptance criteria.
2. Estimate time, compute, storage and third-party costs.
3. Confirm price and spending authority before any purchase or paid API use.
4. Create a task plan and checkpoint.
5. Build in an isolated workspace.
6. Run tests and security checks.
7. Deliver files, instructions, evidence and known limitations.
8. Record outcome and customer-approved feedback.

Do not promise guaranteed results, legal compliance or uptime without evidence.
