# Copyright, Licensing and Reuse

Reading a publicly reachable page does not grant ownership or unrestricted reuse rights.

1. Prefer public-domain works, permissively licensed datasets, open-access publications, official documentation and APIs.
2. Record the exact license or terms URL and version/date where available.
3. Preserve attribution and required notices.
4. Respect non-commercial, share-alike, attribution, database and redistribution conditions.
5. Do not reproduce entire copyrighted books, articles, course materials or paid datasets in the knowledge store.
6. Store concise factual notes, metadata, short necessary quotations and links rather than full expressive works unless the license or permission allows it.
7. For ambiguous or restrictive terms, store bibliographic metadata and a link only, then request legal review before reuse or redistribution.
8. Separate internal research use from publication, commercial delivery and model training permissions.
