# ULUGBEK AI — Business Data and Privacy

Collect only customer data required for the requested service. Define purpose, lawful basis, retention period, access roles and deletion process. Separate customer tenants. Encrypt data in transit and at rest where supported. Do not reuse confidential customer data to train or improve shared models without explicit informed permission. Maintain access logs and incident response steps.
