# Source Trust and Verification

## Evidence tiers
A: primary official source, statute, standards body, original research data.
B: peer-reviewed research, recognized academic institution, reputable technical documentation.
C: established news or specialist publication with editorial process.
D: community posts, personal blogs, forums and social posts.

Tier is not a truth guarantee. Assess relevance, date, methodology, conflicts of interest and corroboration.

## Verification
- For consequential claims, seek a primary source and independent corroboration.
- Record exact supporting passages or section names and the source URL.
- Distinguish fact, estimate, interpretation, prediction and opinion.
- Track contradictions and unresolved uncertainty.
- Mark stale information and schedule re-checks when the topic changes quickly.

## Status values
candidate, corroborated, disputed, stale, rejected. Never promote a candidate to confirmed merely because it appears repeatedly on low-quality sites.
